What is an AI workshop for teachers?

It is a structured professional development program that builds systematic Generative AI skills — not familiarity with individual tools. Teachers leave with a method that holds across subjects, tasks, and whichever AI tool they use next.

The Program

AI Coaching for Educators

Designed for K–12 teachers who are already experimenting with Gen AI but have no shared framework, quality checks, or institutional guidance. The workshop builds professional judgment — how to think with AI before using it — so each teacher leaves with a reliable, repeatable practice rather than a set of borrowed prompts.

Delivered in person or online · Adapted to your school's curriculum and context

The Method

Built on the IMPACT Framework

A six-step methodology developed by Credo Learnings for systematic AI adoption in schools. It trains two distinct skills: using AI as a Thinking Partner, which expands professional judgment before anything is created, and as an Execution Partner, which drafts and iterates once the thinking is clear. The framework is tool-agnostic by design — tools change, the method stays.

Frequently Asked

Direct answers

What is an AI workshop for teachers?

An AI workshop for teachers is a structured professional development program that builds systematic Generative AI skills, rather than familiarity with individual tools. The AI Coaching for Educators program uses the IMPACT Framework to help K–12 teachers in India apply professional judgment to AI use, with consistent workflows and clear guardrails. Teachers leave with a method that holds across tools and subjects. The workshop has been delivered at schools including DPS Vasant Vihar, New Delhi and Indraprastha International School, Dwarka.

Who is Kavleen Kaur Bhatia?

Kavleen Kaur Bhatia is an AI trainer for educators and the founder of Credo Learnings, based in Delhi NCR, India. She spent 15 years at Tata Consultancy Services leading structured software implementation and professional training before moving into education. She holds an MCA and a B.Sc (Hons) in Physics, with executive education from IIT Bombay and AI certification from OpenAI and Wharton Online. She also hosts The Learning Circle, a podcast on K–12 education transformation in India.

What is the IMPACT Framework for AI in education?

The IMPACT Framework is a six-step methodology developed by Credo Learnings for systematic Generative AI adoption in schools. It trains teachers to use AI in two distinct modes: as a Thinking Partner, which expands professional judgment before anything is created, and as an Execution Partner, which drafts and iterates once the thinking is clear. The framework is tool-agnostic by design, so it remains valid as AI tools change.

Does the training cover the CBSE Computational Thinking curriculum?

Yes. Following the CBSE mandate for Computational Thinking and AI curriculum across Classes 3 to 8, teacher readiness training for curriculum delivery is available alongside the core AI professional development program. This covers structured thinking, algorithms, decomposition, and pattern recognition as teachable classroom practice.
